# Section 515.13 - Combined contract.

**Effective:** September 14, 1961

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 605 - 104th General Assembly

The board of township trustees may combine in a single contract the lighting of streets, public ways, roads, highways, or public places as authorized by sections 515.01 to 515.11 , inclusive, of the Revised Code, provided:

(A) That no such contract shall be made to cover a period of more than ten years;

(B) That the procedure for entering into such contract is in accordance with the provisions of section 515.01 of the Revised Code;

(C) That the price per light in the contract is the same throughout the township and for each district within the township.
