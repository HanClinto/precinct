# Section 6111.33 - Dredged material.

**Effective:** October 6, 2017

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 2 - 132nd General Assembly

(A) As used in this section and in sections 6111.32 and 6111.34 of the Revised Code, "dredged material" means material excavated or dredged from a federal navigation channel during harbor or navigation maintenance activities.

(B) No person shall use, manage, or place dredged material in any location except in accordance with the following:

(1) Section 6111.32 of the Revised Code;

(2) Rules adopted under Chapter 6111. of the Revised Code;

(3) A permit issued under any other section of this chapter or under rules adopted under any such section; or

(4) Any other authorization issued by the director of environmental protection.
