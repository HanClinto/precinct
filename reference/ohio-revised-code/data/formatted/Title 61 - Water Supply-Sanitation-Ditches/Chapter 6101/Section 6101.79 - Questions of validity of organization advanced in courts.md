# Section 6101.79 - Questions of validity of organization advanced in courts.

**Effective:** September 21, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 617 - 123rd General Assembly

All cases in which there arises a question of the validity of the organization of conservancy districts shall be advanced as a matter of immediate public interest and concern, and heard in all courts at the earliest practicable moment.

The court shall be open at all times for the purposes of this chapter.
