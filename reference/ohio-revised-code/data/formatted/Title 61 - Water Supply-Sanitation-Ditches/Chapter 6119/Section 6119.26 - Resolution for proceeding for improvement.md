# Section 6119.26 - Resolution for proceeding for improvement.

**Effective:** January 1, 1962

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 262 - 104th General Assembly

After the publication of the notice required by section 6119.25 of the Revised Code, the board of trustees of the regional water and sewer district shall determine whether it will proceed with the proposed improvement or not. If the board decides to proceed with the improvement a resolution for that purpose shall be passed. Such resolution shall contain a statement naming the districts or parts thereof proposed to be constructed, the character of the material to be used, a reference to the plans and specifications, the mode of payment thereof, and shall provide for assessing the cost of the improvement upon the lots and lands in each district as other assessments are levied.
