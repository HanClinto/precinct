# Section 5155.28 - Sub-acute detoxification services.

**Effective:** September 15, 2014

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 483 - 130th General Assembly

(A) As used in this section:

(1) "Nursing facility" has the same meaning as in section 5165.01 of the Revised Code.

(2) "PASRR" means the preadmission screening and annual resident review of individuals with mental illnesses and intellectual disabilities required by the "Social Security Act," 42 U.S.C. 1396r(e)(7).

(B) A county home or district home that is a nursing facility may provide sub-acute detoxification services to residents who have been determined by PASRR to be addicted to opioids. The sub-acute detoxification services shall include monitoring of such residents twenty-four hours a day by health care professionals.
