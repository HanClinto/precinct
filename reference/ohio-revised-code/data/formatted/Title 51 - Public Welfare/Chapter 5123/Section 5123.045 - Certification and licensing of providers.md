# Section 5123.045 - Certification and licensing of providers.

**Effective:** June 30, 2007

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 119 - 127th General Assembly

No person or government entity shall receive payment for providing home and community-based services unless the person or government entity is one of the following:

(A) Certified under section 5123.161 of the Revised Code;

(B) Licensed as a residential facility under section 5123.19 of the Revised Code.
