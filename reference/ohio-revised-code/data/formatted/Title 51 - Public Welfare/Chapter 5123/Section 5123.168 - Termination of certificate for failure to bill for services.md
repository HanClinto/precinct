# Section 5123.168 - Termination of certificate for failure to bill for services.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The director of developmental disabilities shall terminate a supported living certificate if the certificate holder has not billed for supported living for twenty-four consecutive months. To terminate a supported living certificate under this section, the director shall send a notice by certified mail to the certificate holder at the address on file with the department of developmental disabilities explaining why the certificate is terminated.
