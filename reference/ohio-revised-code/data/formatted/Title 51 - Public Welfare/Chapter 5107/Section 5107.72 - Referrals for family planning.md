# Section 5107.72 - Referrals for family planning.

**Effective:** July 1, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 471 - 123rd General Assembly

Each county department of job and family services shall refer a parent participating in Ohio works first whose minor child is a member of the parent's assistance group to any private or public agency, medical doctor, clinic, or other person or organization which can advise the parent on methods of controlling the size and spacing of the parent's family, consistent with the parent's religious and moral views. A county department shall document each referral it makes under this section.
