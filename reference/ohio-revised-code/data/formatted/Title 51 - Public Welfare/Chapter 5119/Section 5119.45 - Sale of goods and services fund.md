# Section 5119.45 - Sale of goods and services fund.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Unless otherwise specifically provided by law, all moneys received by the department of behavioral health from the sale of goods and services, including, but not limited to, shared service agreements with other governmental entities and nongovernmental entities, employee housing and cafeteria receipts, fees for copying services, and sales of other tangible personal property under the department's control, shall be paid into the state treasury to the credit of the sale of goods and services fund, which is hereby created. Moneys received by the department pursuant to section 5119.44 of the Revised Code shall not be paid into the fund. The department shall use the moneys in the fund for paying operating expenses of the department.
