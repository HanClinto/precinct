# Section 5180.53 - [Former R.C. 5101.88, amended and renumbered by H.B. 96, 136th General Assembly, effective 9/30/2025] Kinship support program - definitions.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

As used in sections 5180.531 to 5180.536 of the Revised Code:

(A) "Cost-of-living adjustment" has the same meaning as in section 5107.04 of the Revised Code.

(B) "Kinship caregiver" has the same meaning as in section 5180.50 of the Revised Code.
