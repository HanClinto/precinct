# Section 5180.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

(A) Whoever violates division (B) of section 5180.275 of the Revised Code is guilty of a misdemeanor of the second degree.

(B) Whoever violates section 5180.403 of the Revised Code is guilty of a misdemeanor of the fourth degree.
