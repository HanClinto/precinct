# Section 5180.535 - [Former R.C. 5101.887, amended and renumbered by H.B. 96, 136th General Assembly, effective 9/30/2025] Kinship support program - conditions requiring payments to cease.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Kinship support program payments under section 5180.532 of the Revised Code shall cease when any of the following occur:

(A) The kinship caregiver obtains foster home certification under section 5103.03 of the Revised Code.

(B) In accordance with section 5180.534 of the Revised Code;

(C) Placement with the kinship caregiver is terminated or otherwise ceases.
