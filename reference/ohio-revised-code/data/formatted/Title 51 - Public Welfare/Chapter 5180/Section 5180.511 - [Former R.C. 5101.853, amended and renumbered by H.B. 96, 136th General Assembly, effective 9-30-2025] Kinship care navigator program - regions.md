# Section 5180.511 - [Former R.C. 5101.853, amended and renumbered by H.B. 96, 136th General Assembly, effective 9/30/2025] Kinship care navigator program - regions.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The director of children and youth shall divide the state into not less than five and not greater than twelve regions, for the kinship care navigator program under section 5180.51 of the Revised Code. The director shall take the following into consideration when establishing the regions:

(A) The population size;

(B) The estimated number of kinship caregivers;

(C) The expertise of kinship navigators;

(D) Any other factor the director considers relevant.
