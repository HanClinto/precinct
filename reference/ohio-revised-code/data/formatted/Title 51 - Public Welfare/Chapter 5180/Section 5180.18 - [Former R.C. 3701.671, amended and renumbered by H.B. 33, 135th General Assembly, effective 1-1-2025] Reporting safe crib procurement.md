# Section 5180.18 - [Former R.C. 3701.671, amended and renumbered by H.B. 33, 135th General Assembly, effective 1/1/2025] Reporting safe crib procurement.

**Effective:** January 1, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

The director of children and youth shall require each recipient of a grant the department of children and youth administers that pertains to safe crib procurement to report annually to the department both of the following:

(A) Demographic information specified by the director of children and youth regarding the individuals to whom safe cribs were distributed;

(B) If known, the extent to which distributed cribs are being used.
