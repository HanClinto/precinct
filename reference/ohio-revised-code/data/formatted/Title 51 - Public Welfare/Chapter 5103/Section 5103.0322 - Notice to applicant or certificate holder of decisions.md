# Section 5103.0322 - Notice to applicant or certificate holder of decisions.

**Effective:** January 1, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

On receipt of a recommendation from a public children services agency, private child placing agency, or private noncustodial agency regarding an application for a family foster home or treatment foster home certification under section 5103.03 of the Revised Code, the department of children and youth shall decide whether to issue the certificate. The department shall notify the agency and the applicant of its decision. If the department's decision is different from the recommendation of the agency, the department shall state in the notice the reason that the decision is different from the recommendation.
