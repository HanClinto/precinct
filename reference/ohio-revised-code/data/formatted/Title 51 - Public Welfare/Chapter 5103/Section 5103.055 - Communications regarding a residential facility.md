# Section 5103.055 - Communications regarding a residential facility.

**Effective:** April 3, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 315 - 135th General Assembly

Not later than ninety days after the effective date of this section, the director of children and youth shall adopt rules in accordance with Chapter 119. of the Revised Code to establish both of the following:

(A) A procedure for individuals in a community in which a residential facility is located to communicate concerns, complaints, or other pertinent information to the department regarding the facility;

(B) Standards for tracking and retaining communications received under division (A) of this section.
