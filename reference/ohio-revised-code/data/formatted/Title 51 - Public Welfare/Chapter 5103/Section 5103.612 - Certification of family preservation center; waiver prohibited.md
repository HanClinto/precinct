# Section 5103.612 - Certification of family preservation center; waiver prohibited.

**Effective:** January 1, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

(A) The director of children and youth shall certify the person's family preservation center if the center complies with all of the requirements imposed under section 5103.614 of the Revised Code and all of the rules adopted under section 5103.617 of the Revised Code.

(B) The director shall not issue a waiver to a person of compliance with any of the requirements imposed under this section or any of the rules adopted under section 5103.617 of the Revised Code.
