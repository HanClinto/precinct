# Section 5126.40 - Supported living.

**Effective:** October 12, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 158 - 131st General Assembly

(A) Sections 5126.40 to 5126.47 of the Revised Code do not apply to medicaid-funded supported living.

(B) As used in sections 5126.40 to 5126.47 of the Revised Code, "provider" means a person or government entity certified by the director of developmental disabilities to provide supported living for individuals with developmental disabilities.

(C) On and after July 1, 1995, each county board of developmental disabilities shall plan and develop supported living for individuals with developmental disabilities who are residents of the county in accordance with sections 5126.41 to 5126.47 of the Revised Code.
