# Section 5145.32 - Screening for drugs of abuse.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Every officer or employee of a correctional institution under the control or supervision of the department of rehabilitation and correction, and every contractor, or employee of such contractor, upon entering the grounds of a state correctional institution, shall be subject to screening to prevent the conveyance of drugs of abuse into the institution.
