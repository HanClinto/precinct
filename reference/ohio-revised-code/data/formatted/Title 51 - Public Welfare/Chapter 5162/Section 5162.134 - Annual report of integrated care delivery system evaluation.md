# Section 5162.134 - Annual report of integrated care delivery system evaluation.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Not later than the first day of each July, the medicaid director shall complete a report of the evaluation conducted under section 5164.911 of the Revised Code regarding the integrated care delivery system. The director shall provide a copy of the report to the general assembly in accordance with section 101.68 of the Revised Code. The director also shall make the report available to the public.
