# Section 2151.4228 - Model memorandum of understanding.

**Effective:** January 1, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

(A) The department of children and youth shall create a model memorandum of understanding to provide guidance to public children services agencies and other concerned officials in creating a memorandum of understanding in compliance with sections 2151.4220 to 2151.4226 of the Revised Code.

(B) The model memorandum of understanding shall be updated as the department determines is necessary.
