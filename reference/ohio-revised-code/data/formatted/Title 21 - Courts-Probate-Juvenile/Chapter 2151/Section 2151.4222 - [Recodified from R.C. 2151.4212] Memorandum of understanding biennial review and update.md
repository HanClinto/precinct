# Section 2151.4222 - [Recodified from R.C. 2151.4212] Memorandum of understanding biennial review and update.

**Effective:** May 30, 2022

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 4 - 134th General Assembly

Every official who signed a memorandum of understanding under section 2151.4220 of the Revised Code shall biennially do the following regarding the memorandum:

(A) Review and evaluate the memorandum for necessary updates to terms and procedures;

(B) Update the memorandum's terms and procedures, if the concerned officials determine an update is necessary;

(C) Sign the reviewed memorandum;

(D) Submit the memorandum to the board of county commissioners for approval.
