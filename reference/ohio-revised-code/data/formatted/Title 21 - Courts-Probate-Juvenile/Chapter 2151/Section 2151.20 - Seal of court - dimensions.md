# Section 2151.20 - Seal of court - dimensions.

**Effective:** December 15, 1967

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 164 - 107th General Assembly

Juvenile courts within the probate court shall have a seal which shall consist of the coat of arms of the state within a circle one and one-fourth inches in diameter and shall be surrounded by the words "juvenile court ___________ county."

The seal of other courts exercising the powers and jurisdiction conferred in sections 2151.01 to 2151.54 , inclusive, of the Revised Code, shall be attached to all writs and processes.
