# Section 2151.45 - Emancipated young adult and representative defined.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

As used in sections 2151.45 to 2151.455 of the Revised Code, "emancipated young adult" and "representative" have the same meanings as in section 5180.42 of the Revised Code.
