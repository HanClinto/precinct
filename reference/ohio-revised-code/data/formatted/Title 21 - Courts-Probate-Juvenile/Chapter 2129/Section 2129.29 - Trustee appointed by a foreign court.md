# Section 2129.29 - Trustee appointed by a foreign court.

**Effective:** January 13, 2012

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 124 - 129th General Assembly

If a trustee has been appointed under a foreign will that creates a trust relating to real property situated in this state by a foreign court according to the laws of the foreign jurisdiction, the trustee may execute the trust upon giving bond as provided in section 2129.28 of the Revised Code, and after satisfying the probate court of the county in which the real property or a part of it is situated, by an authenticated record of appointment, that the person or entity has been appointed trustee to execute the trust.
