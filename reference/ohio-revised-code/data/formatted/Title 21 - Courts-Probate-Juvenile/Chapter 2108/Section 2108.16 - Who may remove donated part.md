# Section 2108.16 - Who may remove donated part.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 196 - 135th General Assembly

(A) Except as provided in division (B) of this section, a physician or technician may remove a donated part from the body of a donor that the physician or technician is qualified to remove.

(B) Neither the physician, certified nurse-midwife, clinical nurse specialist, or certified nurse practitioner who attends the decedent at death nor the physician, certified nurse-midwife, clinical nurse specialist, or certified nurse practitioner who determines the time of the decedent's death shall participate in the procedures for removing or transplanting a part from the decedent.
