# Section 1546.92 - Effect of child support default on license.

**Effective:** September 14, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 293 - 131st General Assembly

On receipt of a notice pursuant to section 3123.43 of the Revised Code, the chief of the division of parks and watercraft shall comply with sections 3123.41 to 3123.50 of the Revised Code and any applicable rules adopted under section 3123.63 of the Revised Code with respect to a license issued pursuant to this chapter.
