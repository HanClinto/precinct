# Section 1547.072 - Operator of vessel to maintain control.

**Effective:** June 12, 2008

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 271 - 127th General Assembly

No person shall operate or permit the operation of a vessel on the waters in this state without maintaining sufficient control to avoid an incident that results in property damage, physical injury, loss of life, or any combination of them.
