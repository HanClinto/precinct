# Section 1547.74 - Facilities in harbors and connecting waterways.

**Effective:** September 14, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 293 - 131st General Assembly

Facilities in harbors and connecting waterways established under sections 1546.021 , 1547.71 , and 1547.72 of the Revised Code shall be open to all on equal and reasonable terms.
