# Section 1503.271 - Agreements with federal government for forest management projects.

**Effective:** September 30, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 110 - 134th General Assembly

The chief of the division of forestry may enter into agreements with the federal government under 16 U.S.C. 2113a or other applicable federal statutes for the purpose of forest management projects, including timber sales.
