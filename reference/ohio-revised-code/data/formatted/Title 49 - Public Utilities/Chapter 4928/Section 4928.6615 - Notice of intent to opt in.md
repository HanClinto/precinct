# Section 4928.6615 - Notice of intent to opt in.

**Effective:** September 12, 2014

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 310 - 130th General Assembly

Any customer electing to opt in under section 4928.6614 of the Revised Code shall do so by providing a written notice of intent to opt in to the electric distribution utility from which it receives service and submitting a complete copy of the opt-in notice to the secretary of the public utilities commission. The notice shall include all of the following:

(A) A statement indicating that the customer has elected to opt in;

(B) The effective date of the election to opt in;

(C) The account number for each customer account to which the opt in shall apply;

(D) The physical location of the customer's load center.
