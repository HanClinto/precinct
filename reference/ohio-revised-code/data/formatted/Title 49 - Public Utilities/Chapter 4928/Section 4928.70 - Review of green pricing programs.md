# Section 4928.70 - Review of green pricing programs.

**Effective:** September 10, 2012

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 315 - 129th General Assembly

(A) The public utilities commission may periodically review any green pricing program offered in this state as part of competitive retail electric service. At the conclusion of a review, the commission may make recommendations to improve or expand the program subject of the review.

(B) The commission shall adopt rules necessary to carry out purposes of this section.
