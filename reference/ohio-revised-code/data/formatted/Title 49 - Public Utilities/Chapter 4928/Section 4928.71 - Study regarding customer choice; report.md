# Section 4928.71 - Study regarding customer choice; report.

**Effective:** September 10, 2012

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 315 - 129th General Assembly

The public utilities commission shall study whether increased energy efficiency, demand response, generation, and transmission provide increased opportunities for customer choice. The commission shall include in the study an evaluation of emerging technologies. The commission shall commence the study not later than eighteen months after the effective date of this section. At the conclusion of the study, the commission shall prepare a report of its findings and make the report available on its web site.
