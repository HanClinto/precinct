# Section 4928.80 - Rate schedule applicable to county fairs and agricultural societies.

**Effective:** October 22, 2019

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 6 - 133rd General Assembly

(A) Each electric distribution utility shall file with the public utilities commission a rate schedule applicable to county fairs and agricultural societies that includes either of the following:

(1) A fixed monthly service fee;

(2) An energy charge on a kilowatt-hour basis.

(B) The minimum monthly charge shall not exceed the fixed monthly service fee and the customer shall not be subject to any demand-based riders.

(C) The electric distribution utility shall be eligible to recover any revenue loss associated with customer migration to this new rate schedule.
