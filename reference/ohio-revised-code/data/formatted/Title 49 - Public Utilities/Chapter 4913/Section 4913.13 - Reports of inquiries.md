# Section 4913.13 - Reports of inquiries.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 378 - 130th General Assembly

The staff of the public utilities commission shall make a report of each inquiry conducted under section 4913.09 of the Revised Code available to the underground technical committee, created under section 3781.34 of the Revised Code. The report shall contain any admission made under section 4913.07 of the Revised Code by the person who is the subject of the inquiry. This report shall not contain a recommendation as to the imposition of a fine or penalty or as to whether no enforcement action should be taken.
