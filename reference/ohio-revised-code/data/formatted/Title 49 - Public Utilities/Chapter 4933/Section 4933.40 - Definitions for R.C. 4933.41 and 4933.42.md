# Section 4933.40 - Definitions for R.C. 4933.41 and 4933.42.

**Effective:** September 30, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 201 - 134th General Assembly

As used in sections 4933.41 to 4933.42 of the Revised Code:

(A) "Political subdivision" means a township, county, or municipal corporation.

(B) "Competitive retail natural gas service," "natural gas company," "distribution service," and "retail natural gas service" have the same meanings as in section 4929.01 of the Revised Code.

(C) "Energy dealer" has the same meaning as in section 5117.01 of the Revised Code.

(D) "Propane" has the same meaning as in section 936.01 of the Revised Code.
