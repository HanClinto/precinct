# Section 4906.21 - Decommissioning plan for solar and wind generation required.

**Effective:** October 11, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 52 - 134th General Assembly

(A) For the purposes of this section and sections 4906.21 to 4906.232 of the Revised Code:

(1) "Applicant" means an applicant for a certificate, or a material amendment to an existing certificate, from the power siting board to construct, operate, or maintain a utility facility, and includes any subsequent person to whom the certificate is transferred.

(2) "Material amendment" and "utility facility" have the same meanings as in section 303.57 of the Revised Code.

(B) At least sixty days prior to the commencement of construction of a utility facility, the applicant shall submit a comprehensive decommissioning plan for review and approval by the power siting board.
