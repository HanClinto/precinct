# Section 3773.37 - Photograph with identification of applicant to be taken.

**Effective:** September 3, 1996

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 240 - 121st General Assembly

The Ohio athletic commission shall cause a photograph with identification of any person signing the application for a license under section 3773.35 or 3773.41 of the Revised Code to be taken in duplicate and filed with the commission.. For purposes of this section, the commission may allow a photograph with identification to be a photocopy of a valid commercial driver's license issued under Chapter 4506. or a driver's license issued under Chapter 4507. of the Revised Code.
