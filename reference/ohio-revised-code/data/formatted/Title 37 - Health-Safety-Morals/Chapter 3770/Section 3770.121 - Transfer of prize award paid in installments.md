# Section 3770.121 - Transfer of prize award paid in installments.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Any state lottery commission rules allowing lottery prize awards to be paid in installments also shall allow a prize winner who is being paid a prize award in that manner to transfer the remainder of the prize award, subject to each of the following conditions:

(A) Payments of the prize award transferred shall be subject to the withholding or deduction of any amounts that are required to be withheld or deducted under section 3119.80 , 3119.81 , 3121.02 , 3121.03 , 3123.06 , 3770.071 , or 5747.062 of the Revised Code.

(B) Only one transfer is permitted under this section with respect to any single prize award unless a greater number of permitted transfers has been specified by the commission in the rules.
