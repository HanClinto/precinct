# Section 3746.041 - Applicability of R.C. Chapter 4796.

**Effective:** December 29, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 131 - 134th General Assembly

The director of environmental protection shall issue an environmental professional certification provided for under division (B)(5) of section 3746.04 of the Revised Code in accordance with Chapter 4796. of the Revised Code if an applicant either holds a certification or license in another state, or the applicant has satisfactory work experience, a government certification, or a private certification as described in that chapter as an environmental professional in a state that does not issue that certification.
