# Section 3701.47 - Standard tests for syphilis and gonorrhea.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 196 - 135th General Assembly

As used in sections 3701.46 to 3701.50 of the Revised Code, the standard tests for syphilis and gonorrhea are tests approved by the department of health, and shall be made at a laboratory approved to make such tests by the department. Such tests as are required shall, on request of the physician, certified nurse-midwife, clinical nurse specialist, or certified nurse practitioner submitting the specimens, be made without charge by the department.
