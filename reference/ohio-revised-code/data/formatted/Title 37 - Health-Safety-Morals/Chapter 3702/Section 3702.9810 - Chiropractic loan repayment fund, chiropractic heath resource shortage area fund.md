# Section 3702.9810 - Chiropractic loan repayment fund, chiropractic heath resource shortage area fund.

**Effective:** November 22, 2020

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 151 - 133rd General Assembly

The director of health may accept gifts of money from any source for the implementation and administration of sections 3702.98 to 3702.9810 of the Revised Code.

The director shall pay all gifts accepted under this section into the state treasury, to the credit of the chiropractic health resource shortage area fund, which is hereby created, and all damages collected under division (B)(4) of section 3702.986 of the Revised Code, into the state treasury, to the credit of the chiropractic loan repayment fund, which is hereby created.

The director shall use the chiropractic health resource shortage area and chiropractic loan repayment funds for the implementation and administration of sections 3702.98 to 3702.9810 of the Revised Code.
