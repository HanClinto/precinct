# Section 3772.25 - Activities not subject to chapter.

**Effective:** September 10, 2010

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 519 - 128th General Assembly

The following are not subject to, or limited by, the requirements of this chapter or Section 6(C) of Article XV, Ohio Constitution:

(A) Charitable gaming authorized by Chapter 2915. of the Revised Code;

(B) Charitable bingo authorized by Section 6 of Article XV, Ohio Constitution, and as authorized by Chapter 2915. of the Revised Code;

(C) Lottery games as authorized by Section 6 of Article XV, Ohio Constitution; and

(D) Pari-mutuel wagering authorized by Chapter 3769. of the Revised Code.
