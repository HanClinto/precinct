# Section 3736.021 - Auxiliary containers.

**Effective:** September 30, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 110 - 134th General Assembly

A person may use an auxiliary container for purposes of commerce or otherwise.

Nothing in this section shall be construed to prohibit or limit the authority of any county, municipal corporation, or solid waste management district to implement a voluntary recycling program.
