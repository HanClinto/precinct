# Section 3727.35 - Submitting hospital price lists to director of health.

**Effective:** April 3, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 173 - 135th General Assembly

Each time a hospital updates a list or internet-based price estimator tool as required under sections 3727.33 and 3727.34 of the Revised Code, the hospital shall submit the updated list or a description of the updates to the internet-based price estimator tool to the director of health. The director shall prescribe the form in which the updated list or description is to be submitted.
