# Section 3727.381 - Hospital price transparency fund.

**Effective:** April 3, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 173 - 135th General Assembly

There is hereby created in the state treasury the hospital price transparency fund, consisting of administrative penalties collected under section 3727.38 of the Revised Code. The director of health shall administer the fund. The amounts deposited shall be used for purposes of administering and enforcing sections 3727.31 to 3727.40 of the Revised Code, except that the director may use a portion for purposes of informing the public about the availability of hospital price information and other consumer rights under those sections.
