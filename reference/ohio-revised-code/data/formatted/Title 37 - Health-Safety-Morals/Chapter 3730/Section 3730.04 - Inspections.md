# Section 3730.04 - Inspections.

**Effective:** January 12, 1998

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 25 - 122nd General Assembly

A board of health shall conduct at least one inspection of a business prior to approving the business under section 3730.03 of the Revised Code to offer tattooing or body piercing services. The board may conduct additional inspections as necessary for the approval process. A board of health may inspect an approved business at any time the board considers necessary. In an inspection, a board of health shall be given access to the business's premises and to all records relevant to the inspection.
