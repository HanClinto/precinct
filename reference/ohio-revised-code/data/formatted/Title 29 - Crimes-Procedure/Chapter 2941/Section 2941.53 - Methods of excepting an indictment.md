# Section 2941.53 - Methods of excepting an indictment.

**Effective:** October 1, 1953

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 100th General Assembly

An accused may except to an indictment by:

(A) A motion to quash;

(B) A plea in abatement;

(C) A demurrer.
