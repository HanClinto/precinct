# Section 4141.56 - Report.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The director of job and family services annually shall prepare a report and submit it by the first day of August to the governor, the president and minority leader of the senate, and the speaker and the minority leader of the house of representatives. The report shall discuss the utilization of the SharedWork Ohio program created under section 4141.50 of the Revised Code. The director shall include in that report the number of employers and employees participating in the program, the amount of shared work compensation paid under the program during the immediately preceding year, and any other information the director considers to be relevant.
