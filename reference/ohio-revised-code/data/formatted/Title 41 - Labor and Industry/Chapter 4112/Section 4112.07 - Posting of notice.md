# Section 4112.07 - Posting of notice.

**Effective:** July 21, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 33 - 136th General Assembly

Every person subject to division (A), (B), (C), (D), or (E) of section 4112.02 of the Revised Code shall post in a conspicuous place or places on the person's premises, or on the internet in a manner that is accessible to the public, a notice to be prepared or approved by the commission that shall set forth excerpts of this chapter and other relevant information that the commission deems necessary to explain this chapter.
