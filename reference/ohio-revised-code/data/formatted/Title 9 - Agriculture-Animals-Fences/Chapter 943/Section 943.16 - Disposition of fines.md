# Section 943.16 - Disposition of fines.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

All fines imposed and collected under section 943.99 of the Revised Code shall be credited to the animal and consumer protection fund created in section 943.26 of the Revised Code.
