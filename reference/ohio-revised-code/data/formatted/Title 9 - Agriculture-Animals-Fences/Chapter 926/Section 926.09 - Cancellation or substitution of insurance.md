# Section 926.09 - Cancellation or substitution of insurance.

**Effective:** July 1, 1983

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 770 - 114th General Assembly

No person licensed under sections 926.04 to 926.06 of the Revised Code shall cancel an approved insurance coverage without the prior written approval of the director of agriculture and his approval of a substitute insurance coverage.

No insurance company shall cancel an insurance policy covering the agricultural commodity inventory of a licensed handler without written notice to the director at least thirty days prior to the effective date of cancellation.
