# Section 925.30 - Notice of detention of container not properly marked.

**Effective:** January 14, 1972

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 712 - 109th General Assembly

(A) Whenever the director of agriculture finds or has cause to believe that any container of any fruit or vegetable is not marked in accordance with sections 925.21 to 925.32 , inclusive, of the Revised Code, he shall affix to such container a tag or other appropriate marking:

(1) Giving notice that such container is or is suspected of not being properly marked and has been detained;

(2) Warning all persons not to remove or dispose of such container by sale or otherwise until written permission for removal or disposal is given by the director or a court of competent jurisdiction.

(B) No person shall remove or dispose of any container of fruits or vegetables described in a notice of detention given by the director.
