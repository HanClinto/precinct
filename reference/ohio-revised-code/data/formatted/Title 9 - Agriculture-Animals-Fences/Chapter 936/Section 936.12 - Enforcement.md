# Section 936.12 - Enforcement.

**Effective:** October 17, 2019

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 166 - 133rd General Assembly

The propane council may institute an action at law or in equity that appears necessary to enforce compliance with this chapter, a procedure established under it, or a marketing program established under it.
