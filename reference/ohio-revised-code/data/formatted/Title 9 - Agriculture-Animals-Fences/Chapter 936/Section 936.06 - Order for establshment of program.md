# Section 936.06 - Order for establshment of program.

**Effective:** October 17, 2019

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 166 - 133rd General Assembly

When the retailers who vote in a referendum held under section 936.05 of the Revised Code favor a proposed marketing program, the propane council shall order the program established.
