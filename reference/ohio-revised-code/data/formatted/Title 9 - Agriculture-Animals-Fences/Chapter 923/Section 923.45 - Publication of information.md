# Section 923.45 - Publication of information.

**Effective:** June 30, 2005

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 66 - 126th General Assembly

The director of agriculture may publish annually in such form as the director considers proper:

(A) Information concerning the sale of commercial feed, including any production and use data the director considers advisable, provided that the data does not disclose the operation of any manufacturer or distributor;

(B) A comparison of the analyses of official samples of commercial feeds distributed in this state with the guaranteed analyses on the label.
