# Section 3930.11 - Annual statement.

**Effective:** January 5, 1988

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 117th General Assembly

The Ohio commercial insurance joint underwriting association shall file in the office of the superintendent of insurance, annually on or before the first day of March, a statement that shall contain information with respect to its transactions, conditions, operations, and affairs during the preceding calendar year. Such statement shall contain such matters and information as are prescribed and shall be in such form as is approved by the superintendent.
