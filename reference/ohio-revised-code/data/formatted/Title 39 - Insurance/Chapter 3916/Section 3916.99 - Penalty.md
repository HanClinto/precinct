# Section 3916.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** September 11, 2008

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 404 - 127th General Assembly

(A) Whoever violates section 3916.02 of the Revised Code is guilty of a felony of the third degree.

(B) Whoever violates division (A) of section 3916.171 of the Revised Code is guilty of a violation of section 2913.02 of the Revised Code.
