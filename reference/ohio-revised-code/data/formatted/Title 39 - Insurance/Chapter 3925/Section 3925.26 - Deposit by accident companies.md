# Section 3925.26 - Deposit by accident companies.

**Effective:** October 3, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

When a company organized under section 3925.25 of the Revised Code desires to do business in another state, by the laws of which, to qualify it therefor, it must make a deposit of securities assigned in trust for the benefit of its policyholders with an officer of this state, the superintendent of insurance shall receive such deposit and issue therefor to the company a receipt, giving a pertinent description of the securities and a certificate of their market value. Such company may exchange these securities for other like securities, in whole or in part, as far as its business requires, and it may wholly withdraw them if it discontinues business in such other state.
