# Section 3919.14 - Separate annual statements.

**Effective:** January 1, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 339 - 133rd General Assembly

A company or association organized under section 3919.01 of the Revised Code amending its articles of incorporation and its constitution and bylaws is subject to sections 3919.11 and 3919.12 of the Revised Code as to its organization and government, and it shall make separate annual statements to the superintendent of insurance of the business transacted by it under the assessment plan, as required by section 3919.16 of the Revised Code, and of the business transacted by it under the level premium or legal reserve plan, as required by section 3907.19 of the Revised Code.
