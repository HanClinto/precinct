# Section 3919.29 - Restrictions on issuance of policies.

**Effective:** March 31, 2003

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 245 - 124th General Assembly

No corporation, company, or association organized under section 3919.01 of the Revised Code shall issue a certificate or policy to any person, until such person has first been subjected to a thorough medical examination by a physician, a physician assistant, a clinical nurse specialist, a certified nurse practitioner, or a certified nurse-midwife and found to be a good risk, nor shall it issue a certificate or policy to any person above the age of sixty-five years or under the age of fifteen years. Any written documentation of the physical examination shall be completed by the individual who conducted the examination.

This section, in respect to the age and medical examination of persons to whom certificates or policies may issue, does not apply to such corporations, companies, or associations doing purely accident business.
