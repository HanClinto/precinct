# Section 3918.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** January 9, 1961

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 104th General Assembly

Whoever violates sections 3918.01 to 3918.13 , inclusive, of the Revised Code, shall be fined not less than one hundred nor more than one thousand dollars.

The penalty provided in this section is in addition to any other penalty provided by law.
