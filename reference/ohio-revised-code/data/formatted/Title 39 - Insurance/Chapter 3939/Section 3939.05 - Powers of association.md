# Section 3939.05 - Powers of association.

**Effective:** October 1, 1953

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 100th General Assembly

An association organized under section 3939.01 of the Revised Code shall be considered as a body corporate for all the purposes set forth in the certificate required by section 3939.02 of the Revised Code, and may sue and be sued, and plead and be impleaded, in all courts of law and equity, but in no instance shall the power to insure against losses by fire or tornadoes be exercised to other than members of such association.
