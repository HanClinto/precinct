# Section 3301.95 - Best practices for educational stability.

**Effective:** April 3, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 315 - 135th General Assembly

On at least an annual basis, the department of education and workforce shall provide all school districts with best practices to help ensure the educational stability of students who are in the custody of a public children services agency or private child placing agency.
