# Section 3315.061 - Funds for studies and surveys.

**Effective:** August 9, 1963

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 99 - 105th General Assembly

The board of education of any school district may expend funds for the purpose of conducting, or entering into contracts for, studies or surveys pertaining to school district organization and building needs, curriculum and instructional needs, and needs for improved or additional services that may be rendered by such board. The board of education of any school district may publish reports prepared in connection with such activities.
