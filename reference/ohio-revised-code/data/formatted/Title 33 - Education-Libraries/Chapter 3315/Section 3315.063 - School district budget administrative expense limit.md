# Section 3315.063 - School district budget administrative expense limit.

**Effective:** March 20, 2026

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 184 - 136th General Assembly

No board of education of any city, local, exempted village, or joint vocational school district shall expend more than fifteen per cent of the board's annual operating budget on administrative salaries and benefits and other costs associated with the district's administrative offices.
