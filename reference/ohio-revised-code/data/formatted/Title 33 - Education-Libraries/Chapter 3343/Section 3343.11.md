# Section 3343.11

**Effective:** June 15, 2026

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 450 - 136th General Assembly

The board of trustees of Central state university shall enter into an agreement with either the department of administrative services or another governmental third party under which the Central state university board of trustees transfers oversight authority for the management of the university's facilities to the department or other governmental third party.
