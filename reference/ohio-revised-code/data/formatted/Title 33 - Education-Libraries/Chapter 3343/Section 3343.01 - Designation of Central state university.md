# Section 3343.01 - Designation of Central state university.

**Effective:** November 5, 1965

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 395 - 106th General Assembly

The educational institution designated Central state college by volume 124, Ohio Laws, page 67, shall be known as "Central state university."
