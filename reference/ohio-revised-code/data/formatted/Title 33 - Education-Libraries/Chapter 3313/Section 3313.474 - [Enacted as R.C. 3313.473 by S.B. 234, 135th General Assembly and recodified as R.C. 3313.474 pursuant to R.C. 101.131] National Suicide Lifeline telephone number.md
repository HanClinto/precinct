# Section 3313.474 - [Enacted as R.C. 3313.473 by S.B. 234, 135th General Assembly and recodified as R.C. 3313.474 pursuant to R.C. 101.131] National Suicide Lifeline telephone number.

**Effective:** April 9, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 234 - 135th General Assembly

Each city, local, and exempted village school district and chartered nonpublic school serving any of grades nine through twelve shall include the telephone number for the national suicide and crisis lifeline, "988" or its successor, on all of the following, if provided or used by the school:

(A) Each student identification card issued after the effective date of this section ;

(B) Each planner issued to a student after the effective date of this section ;

(C) Any electronic portal administered by the district or school that may be accessed by students.
