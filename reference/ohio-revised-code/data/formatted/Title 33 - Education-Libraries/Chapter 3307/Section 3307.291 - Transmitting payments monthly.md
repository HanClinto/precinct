# Section 3307.291 - Transmitting payments monthly.

**Effective:** July 13, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 123rd General Assembly

The treasurer of each employer, on receipt from the employer of the voucher for deductions from the compensation of teachers and for the contributions of the employer, shall transmit monthly or at such times as the state teachers retirement board designates the amounts specified in such voucher to the secretary of the board. The secretary, after making a record of all such receipts, shall pay them to the treasurer of state for use according to this chapter.
