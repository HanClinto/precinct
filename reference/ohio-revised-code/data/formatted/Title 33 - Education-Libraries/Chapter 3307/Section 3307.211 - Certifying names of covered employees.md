# Section 3307.211 - Certifying names of covered employees.

**Effective:** July 13, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 123rd General Assembly

During September of each year, or at such other time as the state teachers retirement board approves, each employer shall certify to the board the names of all teachers to whom this chapter applies.
