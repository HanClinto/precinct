# Section 3307.214 - Information to new employees.

**Effective:** January 7, 2013

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 342 - 129th General Assembly

On receipt of notice under section 3307.212 of the Revised Code of the employment of a new teacher, the state teachers retirement system shall inform the teacher of the requirements of section 3307.25 of the Revised Code.
