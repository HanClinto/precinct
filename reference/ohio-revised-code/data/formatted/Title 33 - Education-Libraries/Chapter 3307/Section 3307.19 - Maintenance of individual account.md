# Section 3307.19 - Maintenance of individual account.

**Effective:** April 1, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 535 - 123rd General Assembly

The state teachers retirement board shall provide for the maintenance of an individual account for each contributor showing the amount of the contributor's contributions and any accumulations thereon.
