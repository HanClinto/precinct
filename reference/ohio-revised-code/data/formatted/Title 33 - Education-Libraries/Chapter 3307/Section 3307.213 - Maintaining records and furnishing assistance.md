# Section 3307.213 - Maintaining records and furnishing assistance.

**Effective:** July 13, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 123rd General Assembly

Each employer shall keep such records and shall furnish such information and assistance to the state teachers retirement board as it requires in the discharge of its duties.
