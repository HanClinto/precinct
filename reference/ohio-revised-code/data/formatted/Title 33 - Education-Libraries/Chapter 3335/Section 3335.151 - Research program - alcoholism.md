# Section 3335.151 - Research program - alcoholism.

**Effective:** January 9, 1961

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 104th General Assembly

There is hereby established within the college of medicine of Ohio state university, a research program in alcoholism. In order to carry out such program, the college of medicine is hereby empowered to:

(A) Make studies and investigations concerning causes of alcoholism, practicable methods of prevention, effectiveness of different methods of care and treatment, and encourage, direct, and coordinate research in the field of alcoholism;

(B) Establish and operate, or contract for facilities within the limitations of available funds, for the examination, evaluation, treatment, rehabilitation, and care of alcoholics.
