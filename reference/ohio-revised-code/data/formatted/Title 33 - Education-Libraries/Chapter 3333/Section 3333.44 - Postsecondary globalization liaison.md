# Section 3333.44 - Postsecondary globalization liaison.

**Effective:** September 29, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 64 - 131st General Assembly

The chancellor of higher education shall designate a postsecondary globalization liaison to work with state institutions of higher education, as defined in section 3345.011 of the Revised Code, other state agencies, and representatives of the business community to enhance the state's globalization efforts.

The chancellor may designate a person already employed by the chancellor as the liaison.
