# Section 3333.0415 - Degree and credential performance targets.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Not later than December 31, 2025, the chancellor of higher education, in collaboration with the department of education and workforce and the governor's office of workforce transformation, shall establish the level of attainment necessary to achieve identified performance targets across a range of degrees and credentials.
