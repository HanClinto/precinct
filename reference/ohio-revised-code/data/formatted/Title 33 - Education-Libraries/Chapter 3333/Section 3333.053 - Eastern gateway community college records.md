# Section 3333.053 - Eastern gateway community college records.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The chancellor of higher education shall serve indefinitely as the records custodian for the eastern gateway community college upon that college ceasing operations. However, the chancellor may enter into an agreement authorizing a third party to serve as the records custodian in the chancellor's place.
