# Section 3333.012 - References to Ohio board of regents, chancellor.

**Effective:** October 3, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

Whenever the term "Ohio board of regents" is used, referred to, or designated in any statute, rule, contract, grant, or other document, the use, reference, or designation shall be construed to mean the "chancellor of higher education," unless another section of law expressly provides otherwise.

Whenever the term "chancellor of the Ohio board of regents" or "chancellor" is used, referred to, or designated in any statute, rule, contract, grant, or other document, the use, reference, or designation shall be construed to mean the chancellor of higher education.
