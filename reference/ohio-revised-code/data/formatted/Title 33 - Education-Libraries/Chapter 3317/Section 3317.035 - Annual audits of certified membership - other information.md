# Section 3317.035 - Annual audits of certified membership - other information.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The auditor of state may conduct annual audits of the information certified under section 3317.03 of the Revised Code.
