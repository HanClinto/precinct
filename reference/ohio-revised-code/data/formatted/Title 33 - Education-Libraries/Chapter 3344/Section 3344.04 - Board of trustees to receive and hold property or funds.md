# Section 3344.04 - Board of trustees to receive and hold property or funds.

**Effective:** December 18, 1964

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 2 - 105th General Assembly

The board of trustees of Cleveland state university may receive and hold in trust, for the use and benefit of the university, any grant or devise of land, and any donation or bequest of money or other personal property, to be applied to the general or special use of the university, unless otherwise directed in the donation or bequest. The board of trustees of Cleveland state university may make and enter into all contracts and agreements necessary or incidental to the operation of such university.
