# Section 1907.43 - Award, judgment, and execution.

**Effective:** March 17, 1987

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 158 - 116th General Assembly

When the arbitrators mentioned in section 1907.42 of the Revised Code convene and are qualified, they shall hear and determine the cause, make out their award in writing, and return it to the judge of the county court. The award is valid when signed by any two of the arbitrators. The judge shall enter such the award on his docket, render judgment on it, and issue execution, as in other cases.
