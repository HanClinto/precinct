# Section 5573.09 - All costs may be paid from proceeds of tax levy.

**Effective:** October 1, 1953

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 100th General Assembly

The board of township trustees, upon a unanimous vote, may, without a petition therefor, order that all compensation, damages, and costs of constructing any road improvement be paid out of the proceeds of any levy for road purposes on the grand duplicate of the township, or out of any road improvement fund available therefor.
