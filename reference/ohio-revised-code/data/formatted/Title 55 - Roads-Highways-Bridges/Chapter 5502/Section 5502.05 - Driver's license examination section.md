# Section 5502.05 - Driver's license examination section.

**Effective:** September 10, 2012

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 487 - 129th General Assembly

There is hereby created in the department of public safety, a driver's license examination section.

The director of public safety may appoint necessary driver's license examiners and clerical personnel necessary to carry out the duties assigned under this section. The examiners shall be citizens of the United States and residents of the state and shall have such additional qualifications as the director prescribes.

The salaries and classifications of examiners and personnel shall be fixed in accordance with section 124.15 or 124.152 of the Revised Code.
