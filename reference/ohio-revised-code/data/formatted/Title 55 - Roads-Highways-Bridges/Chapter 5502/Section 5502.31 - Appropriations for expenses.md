# Section 5502.31 - Appropriations for expenses.

**Effective:** July 18, 2008

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 84 - 127th General Assembly

Each political subdivision may make appropriations for the payment of the expenses of its local activities for emergency management incurred by an agency established pursuant to section 5502.271 of the Revised Code or chargeable to that political subdivision by agreement in any county wherein a countywide agency for emergency management has been established pursuant to section 5502.26 of the Revised Code or a regional authority has been established pursuant to section 5502.27 of the Revised Code.
