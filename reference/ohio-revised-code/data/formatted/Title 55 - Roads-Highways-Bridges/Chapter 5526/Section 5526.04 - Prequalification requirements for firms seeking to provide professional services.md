# Section 5526.04 - Prequalification requirements for firms seeking to provide professional services.

**Effective:** June 29, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 73 - 124th General Assembly

The director of transportation may institute prequalification requirements for firms seeking to provide professional services and may require that each prequalified firm maintain a current statement of qualifications with the department of transportation. The prequalification requirements shall be based on the factors set forth in division (D) of section 5526.01 of the Revised Code.
