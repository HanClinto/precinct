# Section 5526.02 - Contracts for professional services.

**Effective:** June 29, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 73 - 124th General Assembly

Notwithstanding Chapter 125. and sections 153.65 to 153.71 of the Revised Code, the director of transportation may employ or enter into contracts with any qualified firm for professional services in accordance with this chapter.
