# Section 5525.061 - Subcontract approval.

**Effective:** July 1, 1983

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 373 - 115th General Assembly

The director of transportation shall not authorize or approve any subcontract pursuant to this chapter unless the subcontract is evidenced in writing and is in conformity with all appllicable state and federal laws and regulations.
