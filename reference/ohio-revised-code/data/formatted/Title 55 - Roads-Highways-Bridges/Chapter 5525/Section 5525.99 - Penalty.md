# Section 5525.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** October 1, 1953

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 100th General Assembly

(A) Whoever violates sections 5525.02 to 5525.09 , inclusive, of the Revised Code, shall be fined not less than one hundred nor more than five hundred dollars.
