# Section 5537.35 - Turnpike rest area flags.

**Effective:** July 1, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 53 - 131st General Assembly

(A) The Ohio turnpike commission shall display the following flags at each service facility that is along the turnpike:

(1) The flag of the United States;

(2) The flag of Ohio;

(3) The flag that depicts the profile of a prisoner of war against the background of a prisoner of war camp watchtower, commonly known as the POW/MIA flag.

(B) In purchasing flags to comply with division (A) of this section, the turnpike commission shall, to the maximum extent possible, conform to the preference requirements of sections 125.09 and 125.11 of the Revised Code and all rules adopted under those sections to ensure the purchase and use of products made in Ohio and the United States.
