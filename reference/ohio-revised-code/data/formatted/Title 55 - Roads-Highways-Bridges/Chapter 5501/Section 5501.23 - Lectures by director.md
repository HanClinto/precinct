# Section 5501.23 - Lectures by director.

**Effective:** September 28, 1973

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 200 - 110th General Assembly

The director of transportation, either in person or through a representative of the department of transportation, may accept invitations to give addresses or lectures before persons interested in transportation improvement and development. In such cases the director, or the person designated by him, shall receive his actual and necessary expenses in addition to his salary.
