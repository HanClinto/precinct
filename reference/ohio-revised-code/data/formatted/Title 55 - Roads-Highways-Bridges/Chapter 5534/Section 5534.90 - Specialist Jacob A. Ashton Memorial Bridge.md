# Section 5534.90 - Specialist Jacob A. Ashton Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 2, 2026

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 27 - 136th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the grand river, located in Lake county and being part of the highway known as interstate route number ninety, shall be known as the "Specialist Jacob A. Ashton Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
