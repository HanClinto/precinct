# Section 5534.10 - Cpl. James E. Blevins Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number seven, running in a northerly and southerly direction from mile marker thirty-two to mile marker twenty-eight in Jefferson county, shall be known as the "Cpl. James E. Blevins Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
