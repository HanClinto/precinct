# Section 5534.658 - Cortland Veterans Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

The bridge spanning Walnut creek, located in the municipal corporation of Cortland in Trumbull county and being a part of the highway known as state route number five, shall be known as the "Cortland Veterans Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
