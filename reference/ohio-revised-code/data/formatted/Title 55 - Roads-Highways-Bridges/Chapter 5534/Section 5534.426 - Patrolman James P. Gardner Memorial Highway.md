# Section 5534.426 - Patrolman James P. Gardner Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed by the Revised Code or otherwise, the road known as United States route number sixty-two, running in an easterly and westerly direction, commencing at the intersection of that route and Western avenue and extending to the intersection of that route and south Mahoning avenue, within the municipal corporation of Alliance only, shall be known as the "Patrolman James P. Gardner Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
