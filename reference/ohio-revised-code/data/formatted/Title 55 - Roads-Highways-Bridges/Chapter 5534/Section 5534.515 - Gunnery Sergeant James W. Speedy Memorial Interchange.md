# Section 5534.515 - Gunnery Sergeant James W. Speedy Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** April 6, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 578 - 134th General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the interchange of the roads known as interstate route number seventy-seven and interstate route number seventy, in Guernsey county, shall be known as the "Gunnery Sergeant James W. Speedy Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
