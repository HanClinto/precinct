# Section 5534.152 - Lance Corporal Michael Stangelo, USMC, Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** October 17, 2019

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 166 - 133rd General Assembly

The bridge spanning state route number twenty-one, located in Lawrence township in Stark county and being a part of the highway known as state route ninety-three, shall be known as "Lance Corporal Michael Stangelo, USMC, Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
