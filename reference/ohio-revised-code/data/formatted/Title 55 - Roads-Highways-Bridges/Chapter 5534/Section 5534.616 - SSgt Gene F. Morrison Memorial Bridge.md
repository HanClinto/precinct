# Section 5534.616 - SSgt Gene F. Morrison Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

The bridge spanning Indian creek, located immediately outside the municipal corporation of Versailles in Darke county, and being a part of the road known as state route number forty-seven, shall be known as the "SSgt Gene F. Morrison Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
