# Section 5534.12 - U.S.M.C. Lance Corporal Dustin R. Fitzgerald "Dusty" Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

That portion of state route two hundred one, running in a northeasterly and southwesterly direction from the intersection of that route with Chambersburg road and the intersection of that route with Taylorsville road in the municipal corporation of Huber Heights, shall be known as the "U.S.M.C. Lance Corporal Dustin R. Fitzgerald "Dusty" Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name."
