# Section 5534.03 - U.S. Army Specialist Justin Helton Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

In addition to the designation of the road known as state route number one hundred twenty-four in section 5533.15 of the Revised Code, that portion of the road that carries the designations of both that state route and state route number thirty-two, commencing at the intersection of that road and state route number two hundred twenty within Pike county and proceeding in an easterly direction to the boundary of Pike county and Jackson county, shall be known as the "U.S. Army Specialist Justin Helton Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
