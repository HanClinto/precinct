# Section 5534.01 - Sgt. James C. Fekete, SF Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** March 23, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 131st General Assembly

The interchange of the road that carries the designations of both United States route number six and state route number two and Rye Beach road located in the municipal corporation of Huron shall be known as the "Sgt. James C. Fekete, SF Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
