# Section 5534.19 - Lieutenant Stephen Byus Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** August 5, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 182 - 131st General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number ninety-three, commencing at the northeast boundary of the municipal corporation of Coalton and proceeding in an easterly direction to the western boundary of the municipal corporation of Wellston, within Jackson county only, shall be known as the "Lieutenant Stephen Byus Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
