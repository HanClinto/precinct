# Section 5534.82 - World War I and World War II Veterans Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2019

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 86 - 132nd General Assembly

The bridge spanning Squaw creek, located in Trumbull county, and being a part of the highway known as state route number one hundred sixty-nine, shall be known as the "World War I and World War II Veterans Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
