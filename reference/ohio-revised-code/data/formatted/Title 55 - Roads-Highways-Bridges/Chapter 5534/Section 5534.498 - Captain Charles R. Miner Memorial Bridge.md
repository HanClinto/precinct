# Section 5534.498 - Captain Charles R. Miner Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** April 6, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 578 - 134th General Assembly

The bridge spanning state route number eleven, located in Fairfield township in Columbiana county and being a part of the highway known as state route one hundred sixty-four, shall be known as "Captain Charles R. Miner Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
