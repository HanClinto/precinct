# Section 5534.08 - Specialist Angel L. Lopez Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, that portion of the highway known as interstate route number seventy-one, commencing at the location where that interstate highway passes over interstate route number four hundred eighty and proceeding in a northerly direction to the interchange of interstate highway seventy-one and west one hundred fiftieth street, within the municipal corporation of Cleveland only, shall be known as the "Specialist Angel L. Lopez Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
