# Section 5534.701 - Peter and Sarah Fossett Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

The bridge connecting the southbound lanes of interstate route number seventy-five and the westbound lanes of interstate route number seventy-four in Hamilton county shall be known as the "Peter and Sarah Fossett Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
