# Section 5534.06 - Sgt. Michael W. Finke, Jr. U.S.M.C. Memorial Walkover.

**Effective:** March 23, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 131st General Assembly

The pedestrian bridge spanning the road known as United States route number six, located in the municipal corporation of Huron and having a northern terminus of south Jim Campbell boulevard and a southern terminus of Adams avenue, shall be known as the "Sgt. Michael W. Finke, Jr. U.S.M.C. Memorial Walkover."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
