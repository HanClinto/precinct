# Section 5534.27 - Army Chief Warrant Officer Donald V. Clark Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** August 5, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 182 - 131st General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number seven, commencing at the intersection of that state route and the road known as West Western Reserve road to the west of that state route and as East Western Reserve road to the east of that state route and proceeding in a northerly direction to the intersection of that state route and United States route number two hundred twenty-four, within Mahoning county only, shall be known as the "Army Chief Warrant Officer Donald V. Clark Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
