# Section 5534.33 - USMC LCpl Stacy (Annie) Dryden Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** August 5, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 182 - 131st General Assembly

That portion of the road known as United States route number sixty-two, commencing at the interchange of that route with interstate route number seventy-seven and proceeding in an easterly direction to the end of the entrance ramp onto United States route number sixty-two from Cleveland avenue northwest, within the municipal corporation of Canton only, shall be known as the "USMC LCpl Stacy (Annie) Dryden Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
