# Section 5534.646 - Brigadier General Charles Young Memorial Historical Corridor.

**Effective:** October 21, 2024

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 253 - 135th General Assembly

(A) In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, all of the following shall be known as the "Brigadier General Charles Young Memorial Historical Corridor":

(1) The northeastbound and southwestbound lanes of United States route number forty-two between the intersection of United States route number forty-two and campus drive and the intersection of United States route number forty-two and United States route number sixty-eight in Greene county;

(2) The northbound and southbound lanes of United States route number sixty-eight between the intersection of that route and United States route number forty-two in Greene county and the intersection of United States route number sixty-eight and United States route number sixty-two in Brown county;

(3) The northbound and southbound lanes of United States route number sixty-two between the intersection of that route and United States route number sixty-eight and the Simon Kenton bridge in Brown county.

(B) The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highways indicating their name.
