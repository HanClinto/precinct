# Section 5534.02 - FN James C. Baumer, USN Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** March 23, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 190 - 131st General Assembly

The interchange of the roads known as state route number two and state route number thirteen located in Huron township in Erie county shall be known as the "FN James C. Baumer, USN Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
