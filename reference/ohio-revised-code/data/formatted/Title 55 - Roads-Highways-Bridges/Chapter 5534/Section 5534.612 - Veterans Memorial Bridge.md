# Section 5534.612 - Veterans Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the Mahoning river, located in the municipal corporation of Newton falls in Trumbull county and being a part of the highway known as state route five hundred thirty-four, shall be known as "Veterans Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
