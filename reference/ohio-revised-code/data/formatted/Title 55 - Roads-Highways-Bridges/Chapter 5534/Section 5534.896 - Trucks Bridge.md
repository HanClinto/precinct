# Section 5534.896 - Trucks Bridge.

**Effective:** June 10, 2020

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 276 - 133rd General Assembly

The bridge that is part of state route number six hundred three, located in the village of Plymouth in Huron and Richland counties, and being a part of the road known as Trux street, shall be known as the "Trucks Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
