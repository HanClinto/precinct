# Section 5534.752 - Damascus Bridge.

**Effective:** June 10, 2020

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 276 - 133rd General Assembly

The bridge spanning the Maumee river in Henry county and being a part of the highway known as state route one hundred nine shall be known as the "Damascus Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
