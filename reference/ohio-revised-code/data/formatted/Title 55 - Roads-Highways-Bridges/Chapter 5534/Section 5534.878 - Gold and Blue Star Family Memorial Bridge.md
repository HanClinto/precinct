# Section 5534.878 - Gold and Blue Star Family Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the St. Joseph river, located in the village of Montpelier in Williams county, and being part of state route number one hundred seven, shall be known as the "Gold and Blue Star Family Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
