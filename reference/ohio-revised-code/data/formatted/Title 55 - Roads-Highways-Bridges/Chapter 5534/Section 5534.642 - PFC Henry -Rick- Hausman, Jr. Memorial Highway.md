# Section 5534.642 - PFC Henry "Rick" Hausman, Jr. Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 20, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 81 - 135th General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the northbound and southbound lanes of interstate route number two hundred seventy, between Cemetery road and Roberts road, in Franklin county, shall be known as the "PFC Henry "Rick" Hausman, Jr. Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
