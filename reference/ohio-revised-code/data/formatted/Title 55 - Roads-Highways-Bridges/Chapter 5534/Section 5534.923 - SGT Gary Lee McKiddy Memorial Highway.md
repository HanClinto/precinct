# Section 5534.923 - SGT Gary Lee McKiddy Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** July 21, 2022

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 291 - 134th General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the portion of the road known as state route number seven hundred twenty-five, running in a westerly and easterly direction, between the intersection of that route and south union road and the intersection of that route and soldiers home road, in Montgomery county only, shall be known as the "SGT Gary Lee McKiddy Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
