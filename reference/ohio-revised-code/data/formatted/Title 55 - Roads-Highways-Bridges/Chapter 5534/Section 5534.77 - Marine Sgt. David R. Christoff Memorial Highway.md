# Section 5534.77 - Marine Sgt. David R. Christoff Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** November 2, 2018

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 347 - 132nd General Assembly

In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the northbound and southbound lanes of state route number sixty-five between the intersections of Elm street and Rosedale place, in the municipal corporation of Rossford in Wood county, shall be known as the "Marine Sgt. David R. Christoff Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
