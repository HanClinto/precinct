# Section 5534.16 - Lance Corporal Ryan E. Miller Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** August 5, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 182 - 131st General Assembly

That portion of interstate route two hundred seventy running in a northerly direction from mile marker thirty-three to mile marker thirty-four shall be known as the "Lance Corporal Ryan E. Miller Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
