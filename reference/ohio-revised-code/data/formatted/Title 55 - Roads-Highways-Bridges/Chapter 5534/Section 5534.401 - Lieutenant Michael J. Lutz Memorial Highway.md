# Section 5534.401 - Lieutenant Michael J. Lutz Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** November 2, 2018

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 347 - 132nd General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number sixty, running in a northerly and southerly direction between the intersection of that route and county road number ninety-seven and the intersection of that route and the southern boundary of the municipal corporation of Zanesville in Muskingum county, shall be known as "Lieutenant Michael J. Lutz Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
