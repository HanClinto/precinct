# Section 5534.608 - Specialist Ronald Gene Johnson Memorial Overpass.

**Effective:** April 6, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 578 - 134th General Assembly

The overpass spanning United States route number two hundred fifty that is part of Johnson drive and that is located in the municipal corporation of Uhrichsville in Tuscarawas county shall be known as the "Specialist Ronald Gene Johnson Memorial Overpass."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
