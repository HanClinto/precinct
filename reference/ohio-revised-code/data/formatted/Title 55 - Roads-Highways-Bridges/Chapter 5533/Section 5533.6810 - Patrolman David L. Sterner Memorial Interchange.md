# Section 5533.6810 - Patrolman David L. Sterner Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** March 22, 2013

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 325 - 129th General Assembly

The interchange of state route number fifteen and state route number thirty-seven in Hancock county shall be known as the "Patrolman David L. Sterner Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
