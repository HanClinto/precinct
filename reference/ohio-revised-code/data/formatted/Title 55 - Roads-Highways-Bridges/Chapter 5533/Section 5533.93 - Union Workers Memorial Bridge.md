# Section 5533.93 - Union Workers Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** May 31, 2010

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 27 - 128th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the Cuyahoga river and the Ohio canal in Cuyahoga county, being part of the highway known as interstate route number four hundred eighty, shall be known as the "Union Workers Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
