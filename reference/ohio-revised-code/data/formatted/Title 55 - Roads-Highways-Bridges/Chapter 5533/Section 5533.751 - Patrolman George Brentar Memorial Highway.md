# Section 5533.751 - Patrolman George Brentar Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 273 - 127th General Assembly

In addition to the designation in section 5533.35 of the Revised Code, the road known as interstate highway number ninety, located within the municipal corporation of Euclid in Cuyahoga county only, shall be known as the "Patrolman George Brentar Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
