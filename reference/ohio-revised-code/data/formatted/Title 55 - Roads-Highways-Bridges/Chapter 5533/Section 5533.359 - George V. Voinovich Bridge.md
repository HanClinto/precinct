# Section 5533.359 - George V. Voinovich Bridge.

**Effective:** August 5, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 182 - 131st General Assembly

In addition to the designation in section 5533.35 of the Revised Code, both the eastbound and westbound bridges that will span the Cuyahoga river in Cuyahoga county and will be part of the highway known as interstate route number ninety shall be known as the "George V. Voinovich Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridges or along their approaches indicating their name.
