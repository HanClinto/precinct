# Section 5533.762 - Sgt. Robert M. Carr Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 273 - 127th General Assembly

The interchange of state route number forty-five and state route number eighty-two located in Champion township in Trumbull county shall be known as the "Sgt. Robert M. Carr Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
