# Section 5533.01 - Markers on state and federal highways at points crossed by Morgan's raiders.

**Effective:** September 29, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 141 - 131st General Assembly

The director of transportation and the director of the Ohio history connection, by agreement, may erect suitable markers on all state and federal highways, at points crossed by Morgan's raiders in 1863.
