# Section 5533.931 - Fred Krum Memorial Interchange.

**Effective:** May 31, 2010

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 27 - 128th General Assembly

The interchange of interstate route seventy-seven and Shuffel street, northwest, located in Jackson township in Stark county, shall be known as the "Fred Krum Memorial Interchange."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
