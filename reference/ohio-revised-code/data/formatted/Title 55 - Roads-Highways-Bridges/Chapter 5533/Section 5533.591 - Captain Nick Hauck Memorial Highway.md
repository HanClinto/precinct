# Section 5533.591 - Captain Nick Hauck Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** September 23, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 65 - 129th General Assembly

That part of the road known as state route seven, located within the municipal corporation of Mingo Junction in Jefferson county only, shall be known as the "Captain Nick Hauck Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
