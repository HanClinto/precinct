# Section 5533.462 - Southern Ohio Veterans Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 36 - 130th General Assembly

The road that will be known as state route number eight hundred twenty-three and having an approximate scheduled completion date of not later than 2020, commencing at the intersection of that route and United States route number twenty-three and proceeding in a southerly direction to the intersection of that state route and United States route number fifty-two in Scioto county only, shall be known as the "Southern Ohio Veterans Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
