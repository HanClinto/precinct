# Section 5533.603 - Freeman C. Thompson Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** September 23, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 65 - 129th General Assembly

The bridge that is part of state route number eight hundred twenty-one, between mile marker number eight and mile marker number nine at mile eight and four-hundredths miles, near the village of Caldwell in Noble county, shall be known as the "Freeman C. Thompson Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
