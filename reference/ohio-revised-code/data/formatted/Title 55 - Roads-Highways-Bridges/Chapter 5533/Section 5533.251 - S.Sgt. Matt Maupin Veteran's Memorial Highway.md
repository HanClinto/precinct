# Section 5533.251 - S.Sgt. Matt Maupin Veteran's Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 243 - 127th General Assembly

In addition to the designation in section 5533.25 of the Revised Code, that portion of the road known as interstate route number two hundred seventy-five located within Clermont county shall be known as the "S.Sgt. Matt Maupin Veteran's Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
