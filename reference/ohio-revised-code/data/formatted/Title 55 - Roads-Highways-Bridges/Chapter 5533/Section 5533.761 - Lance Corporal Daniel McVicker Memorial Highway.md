# Section 5533.761 - Lance Corporal Daniel McVicker Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 273 - 127th General Assembly

That portion of the road known as United States route sixty-two, commencing at the western boundary of the municipal corporation of Alliance and extending in an easterly direction to the Highland Memorial Park Cemetery in the municipal corporation of Beloit, shall be known as the "Lance Corporal Daniel McVicker Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
