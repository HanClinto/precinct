# Section 5533.782 - Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans Bridge.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the Mahoning river, located within the municipal corporation of Niles in Trumbull county and being part of the highway known as state route number forty-six, commencing at the intersection of that state route and the road known as west state street southeast to the west of that state route and as east state street southeast to the east of that state route and proceeding in a southerly direction to the intersection of that state route and the road known as west first street to the west of that state route and as east first street to the east of that state route, shall be known as the "Iraq and Afghanistan Veterans Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
