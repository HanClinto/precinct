# Section 5533.941 - Cpl. Kenneth Tyler Butler Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** July 3, 2012

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 179 - 129th General Assembly

That part of the road known as state route number one hundred seventy, within the municipal corporation of Calcutta in Columbiana county only, shall be known as the "Cpl. Kenneth Tyler Butler Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
