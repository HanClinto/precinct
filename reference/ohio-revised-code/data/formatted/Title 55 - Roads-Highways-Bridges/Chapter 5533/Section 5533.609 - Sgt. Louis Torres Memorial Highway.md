# Section 5533.609 - Sgt. Louis Torres Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** June 10, 2014

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 209 - 130th General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number fifty-eight, commencing at the intersection of that state route and state route number five hundred eleven and proceeding in a northerly direction to the intersection of state route fifty-eight and the road known as Cleveland avenue to the west of that state route and as north ridge road east to the east of that state route, within Lorain county only, shall be known as the "Sgt. Louis Torres Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
