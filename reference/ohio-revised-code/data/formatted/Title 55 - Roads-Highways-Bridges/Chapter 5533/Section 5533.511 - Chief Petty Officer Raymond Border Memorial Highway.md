# Section 5533.511 - Chief Petty Officer Raymond Border Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

In addition to the designation in section 5533.51 of the Revised Code, that portion of United States route number thirty-six, commencing at the intersection of that route and state route number eighty-three and proceeding in an easterly direction to the intersection of that United States route and state route number ninety-three within Coshocton county only, shall be known as the "Chief Petty Officer Raymond Border Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
