# Section 5533.684 - Deputy Lawrence Barnes Memorial Bridge.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 243 - 127th General Assembly

The bridge spanning the North Fork creek at the community of Slate Mills in Ross county and being a part of United States route number fifty west shall be known as the "Deputy Lawrence Barnes Memorial Bridge."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
