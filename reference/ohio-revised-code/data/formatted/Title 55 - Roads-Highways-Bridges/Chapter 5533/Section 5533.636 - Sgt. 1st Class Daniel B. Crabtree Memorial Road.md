# Section 5533.636 - Sgt. 1st Class Daniel B. Crabtree Memorial Road.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 440 - 130th General Assembly

That portion of the road known as state route number six hundred nineteen, commencing at the eastern edge of the municipal corporation of Hartville in Stark county and extending in a westerly direction to the intersection of that road with state route number ninety-three in Summit county, shall be known as the "Sgt. 1st Class Daniel B. Crabtree Memorial Road."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
