# Section 5533.767 - Staff Sgt. Kevin J. Kessler Memorial Highway.

**Effective:** September 23, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 65 - 129th General Assembly

That portion of the road known as United States route number thirty, commencing at the boundary between Canton and Osnaburg townships and proceeding in an easterly direction to the boundary between Osnaburg township and the municipal corporation of East Canton within Stark county only, shall be known as the "Staff Sgt. Kevin J. Kessler Memorial Highway."

The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
