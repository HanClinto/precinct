# Section 4582.49 - Bond laws not applicable to port authority revenue bonds.

**Effective:** July 9, 1982

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 439 - 114th General Assembly

The issuance of port authority revenue bonds under sections 4582.22 to 4582.59 of the Revised Code need not comply with any other law applicable to the issuance of bonds or notes.
