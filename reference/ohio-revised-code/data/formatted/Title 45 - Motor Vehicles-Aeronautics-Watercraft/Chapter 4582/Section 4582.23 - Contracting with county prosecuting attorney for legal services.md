# Section 4582.23 - Contracting with county prosecuting attorney for legal services.

**Effective:** November 2, 2018

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 34 - 132nd General Assembly

The board of directors of a port authority created under section 4582.22 of the Revised Code may contract with the prosecuting attorney of a county, as provided in section 309.09 of the Revised Code, to obtain legal services from the prosecuting attorney.
