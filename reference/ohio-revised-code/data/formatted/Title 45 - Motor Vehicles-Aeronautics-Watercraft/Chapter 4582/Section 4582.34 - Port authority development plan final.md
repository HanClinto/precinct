# Section 4582.34 - Port authority development plan final.

**Effective:** April 7, 2009

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 353 - 127th General Assembly

A plan and any modification, amendment or extension of the plan, when adopted by the board of directors after notice and hearing under section 4582.32 or 4582.33 of the Revised Code shall be final and conclusive and its validity shall be conclusively presumed.
