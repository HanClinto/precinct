# Section 4507.321 - Prohibition against employing minor to operate taxicab.

**Effective:** January 1, 2004

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 123 - 124th General Assembly

(A) Notwithstanding the definition of "chauffeur" in section 4501.01 of the Revised Code, no person shall employ any minor for the purpose of operating a taxicab.

(B) Whoever violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor of the first degree.
