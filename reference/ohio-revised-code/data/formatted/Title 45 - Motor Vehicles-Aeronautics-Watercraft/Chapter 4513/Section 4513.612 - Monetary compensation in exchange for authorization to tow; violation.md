# Section 4513.612 - Monetary compensation in exchange for authorization to tow; violation.

**Effective:** April 6, 2017

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 341 - 131st General Assembly

(A)(1) No towing service shall knowingly offer or provide monetary compensation in exchange for the authorization to tow motor vehicles from a specified location or on behalf of the person to whom the towing service offered or provided the compensation.

(2) Division (A)(1) of this section does not prohibit a towing service from negotiating or reducing towing and storage fees.

(B) Whoever violates division (A) of this section is guilty of a minor misdemeanor.
