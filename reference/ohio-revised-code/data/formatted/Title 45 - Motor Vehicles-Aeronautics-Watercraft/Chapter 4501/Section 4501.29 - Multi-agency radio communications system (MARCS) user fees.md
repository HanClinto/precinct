# Section 4501.29 - Multi-agency radio communications system (MARCS) user fees.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The department of administrative services shall collect user fees from participants in the multi-agency radio communications system (MARCS). The director of administrative services, with the consent of the director of budget and management, shall determine the amount of the user fees and the manner by which the fees shall be collected. All moneys from user fees shall be deposited in the MARCS administration fund, which is hereby created in the state treasury. All investment earnings on moneys in the fund shall be credited to the fund.
