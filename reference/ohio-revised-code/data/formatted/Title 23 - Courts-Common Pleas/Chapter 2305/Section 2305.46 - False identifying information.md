# Section 2305.46 - False identifying information.

**Effective:** September 30, 1976

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1217 - 111th General Assembly

(A) No person, with purpose to deceive, shall provide, wear, use, or possess a false identifying device or identification card.

(B) Whoever violates division (A) of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor of the third degree.
