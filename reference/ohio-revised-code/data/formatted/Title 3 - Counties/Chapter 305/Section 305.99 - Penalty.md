# Section 305.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** July 1, 1996

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 2 - 121st General Assembly

Whoever violates section 305.38 , 305.39 , or 305.41 of the Revised Code is guilty of a minor misdemeanor.
