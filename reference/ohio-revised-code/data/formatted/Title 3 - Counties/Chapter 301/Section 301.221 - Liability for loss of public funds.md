# Section 301.221 - Liability for loss of public funds.

**Effective:** September 13, 2022

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 15 - 134th General Assembly

The treasurer, auditor, or other officer having the duties of a county treasurer or a county auditor, for a county that has adopted a charter under Article X, Ohio Constitution, shall not be held liable for a loss of public funds when the officer has performed all official duties required of the office with reasonable care, but shall be liable only when a loss of public funds results from the officer's negligence or other wrongful act.
