# Section 317.35 - Record of plans and drawings of public buildings.

**Effective:** January 30, 2014

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 72 - 130th General Assembly

(A) The county recorder shall record the plans and drawings filed under section 9.56 of the Revised Code and shall make them available for public inspection.

(B) For services provided, the county recorder shall charge for photocopying ten dollars for the first two pages and two dollars for each page thereafter.
