# Section 1729.99 - Penalty.

**Effective:** August 5, 1998

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 600 - 122nd General Assembly

(A) Whoever violates section 1729.04 of the Revised Code shall be fined not less than five hundred nor more than twenty-five hundred dollars for each offense.

(B) Whoever violates division (A) of section 1729.68 or commits an unfair marketing practice as defined in section 1729.69 of the Revised Code shall be fined not less than five hundred nor more than five thousand dollars for each offense.
