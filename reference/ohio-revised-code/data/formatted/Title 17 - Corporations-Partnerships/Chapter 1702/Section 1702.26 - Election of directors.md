# Section 1702.26 - Election of directors.

**Effective:** April 10, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 597 - 123rd General Assembly

(A) At a meeting of members at which directors are to be elected, only persons nominated as candidates shall be eligible for election as directors.

(B) At all elections of directors the candidates receiving the greatest number of votes shall be elected.
