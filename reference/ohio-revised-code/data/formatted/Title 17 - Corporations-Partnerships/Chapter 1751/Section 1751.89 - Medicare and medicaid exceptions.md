# Section 1751.89 - Medicare and medicaid exceptions.

**Effective:** December 26, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 218 - 129th General Assembly

Sections 1751.77 to 1751.83 of the Revised Code do not apply to either of the following:

(A) Coverage provided to beneficiaries enrolled in the medicare+choice program operated under Title XVIII of the "Social Security Act," 49 Stat. 620 (1935), 42 U.S.C.A. 301, as amended;

(B) Coverage provided to medicaid recipients;

(C) Coverage provided to participants of the children's buy-in program.
