# Section 3121.86 - Administrative rules for case registry.

**Effective:** March 22, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 180 - 123rd General Assembly

The director of job and family services shall adopt rules in accordance with Chapter 119. of the Revised Code that do both of the following:

(A) Establish procedures governing actions required by sections 3121.84 and 3121.85 of the Revised Code;

(B) Designate any additional information that must be placed in the case registry consistent with Title IV-D of the "Social Security Act," 42 U.S.C. 651 et seq., as amended, and any federal regulations adopted under the act.
