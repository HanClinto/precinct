# Section 3121.8910 - Fee for failing to make new hire report.

**Effective:** March 21, 2005

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 200 - 125th General Assembly

An employer that fails to make a new hire report shall be liable to the department of job and family services for a civil penalty of twenty-five dollars for each failure to make a report.

If the failure to make a new hire report is the result of a conspiracy between the employer and the employee not to supply the report or to supply a false or incomplete report, the employer shall be liable for a civil penalty of five hundred dollars for each such failure.
