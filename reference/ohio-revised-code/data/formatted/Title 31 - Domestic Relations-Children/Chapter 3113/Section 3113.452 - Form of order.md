# Section 3113.452 - Form of order.

**Effective:** September 8, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 359 - 131st General Assembly

An order issued in compliance with section 3113.451 of the Revised Code shall include the following:

(A) The name and billing telephone number of the account holder;

(B) The name and contact information of the petitioner to whom the wireless service number or numbers shall be transferred;

(C) Each wireless service number to be transferred to the petitioner.
