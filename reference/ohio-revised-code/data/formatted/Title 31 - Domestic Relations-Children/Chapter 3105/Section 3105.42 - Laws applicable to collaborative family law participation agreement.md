# Section 3105.42 - Laws applicable to collaborative family law participation agreement.

**Effective:** March 22, 2013

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 461 - 129th General Assembly

(A) Sections 3105.41 to 3105.54 of the Revised Code apply to a collaborative family law participation agreement that meets the requirements of section 3105.43 of the Revised Code and is signed on or after the effective date of this section.

(B) A court may not order a party to participate in a collaborative family law process over that party's objection.
