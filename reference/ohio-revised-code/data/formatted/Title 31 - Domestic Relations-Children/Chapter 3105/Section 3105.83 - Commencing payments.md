# Section 3105.83 - Commencing payments.

**Effective:** January 1, 2002

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 535 - 123rd General Assembly

Payments under an order described in section 3105.81 of the Revised Code shall commence as provided under section 145.571 , 742.462 , 3305.21 , 3307.371 , 3309.671 , or 5505.261 of the Revised Code. An alternate payee has no right or privilege under sections 742.01 to 742.61 or Chapter 145., 3305., 3307., 3309., or 5505. of the Revised Code that is not provided in those sections or chapters.

An order described in section 3105.81 of the Revised Code shall not require a public retirement program to take any action or provide any benefit, allowance, or payment not authorized under the law governing the public retirement program.
