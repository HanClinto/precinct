# Section 3109.177 - Primary prevention strategies.

**Effective:** September 29, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 64 - 131st General Assembly

(A) As used in this section and section 3107.178 of the Revised Code, "primary prevention strategies" has the same meaning as in section 3109.13 of the Revised Code.

(B) Each children's advocacy center may annually request funds from the children's trust fund board to conduct primary prevention strategies.
