# Section 126.022 - Estimates of revenues from tobacco master settlement agreement.

**Effective:** June 2, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 192 - 123rd General Assembly

Not later than four weeks after the general assembly convenes in each even-numbered year, the director of budget and management shall prepare and recommend to the general assembly, subject to the concurrence of the governor, estimates of revenues from, or derived from, payments to the state under the tobacco master settlement agreement and expenditures of such revenues for the biennium beginning on the following first day of July. Each state agency affected by such revenues or expenditures shall submit to the director of budget and management any related information the director requires, in such form and at such times as the director prescribes.
