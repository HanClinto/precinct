# Section 145.55 - Members deemed to consent to deductions.

**Effective:** September 21, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 628 - 123rd General Assembly

The deductions provided for in this chapter shall be made notwithstanding that the minimum compensation provided for by law for any contributor is reduced thereby. Every contributor is deemed to consent to the deductions made and provided for in this chapter. Payment less the deductions shall be a complete discharge and acquittance of all claims and demands whatsoever for the services rendered by such person during the period covered by such payment.
