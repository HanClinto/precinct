# Section 147.371 - Duplicate commission.

**Effective:** April 3, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 315 - 135th General Assembly

(A) Upon receipt of a fee of two dollars and submission of the electronic duplicate commission request form, a duplicate commission as notary public shall be issued by the secretary of state.

(B) Upon receipt of a fee of two dollars and submission of the electronic amendment form for a name change under division (B) of section 147.05 of the Revised Code, the secretary of state shall issue a duplicate commission as a notary public.

(C) The secretary of state shall prescribe and make available an electronic duplicate commission request form and an electronic amendment form.
