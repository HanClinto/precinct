# Section 103.41 - Standing committee oversight of medicaid.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The standing committees of the house of representatives and the senate that primarily consider legislation governing the medicaid program shall meet jointly during each session of the general assembly to oversee the medicaid program on a continuing basis.

In odd numbered years, the standing committees shall meet jointly at the call of the chairperson of the senate committee that considers the medicaid program. In even numbered years, the standing committees shall meet jointly at the call of the chairperson of the house of representatives committee that considers the medicaid program.
