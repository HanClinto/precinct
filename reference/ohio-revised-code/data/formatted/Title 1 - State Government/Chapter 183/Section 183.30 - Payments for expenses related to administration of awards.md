# Section 183.30 - Payments for expenses related to administration of awards.

**Effective:** September 29, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 153 - 129th General Assembly

Payments may be made from the biomedical research and technology transfer trust fund for third frontier commission expenses related to the administration of awards made from the fund prior to the effective date of this section. No such payments shall be made after June 30, 2013.
