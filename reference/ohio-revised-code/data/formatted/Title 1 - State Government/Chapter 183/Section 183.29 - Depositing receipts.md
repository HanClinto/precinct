# Section 183.29 - Depositing receipts.

**Effective:** June 2, 2000

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 192 - 123rd General Assembly

The treasurer of state shall, except for any petty cash funds, keep all money received from tobacco master settlement agreement payments or from distributions under this chapter that is needed to meet current demands for the money under this chapter, in public depositories of the active deposits of public moneys of the state, as such terms are used in Chapter 135. of the Revised Code.
