# Section 107.51 - "Agency" and "draft rule" defined; construction of sections.

**Effective:** October 3, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 33 - 135th General Assembly

As used in sections 107.51 to 107.55 of the Revised Code, "agency" and "draft rule" have the meanings defined in section 121.81 of the Revised Code.

Sections 107.51 to 107.55 and 107.61 to 107.63 of the Revised Code are complementary to sections 121.81 to 121.82 of the Revised Code.
