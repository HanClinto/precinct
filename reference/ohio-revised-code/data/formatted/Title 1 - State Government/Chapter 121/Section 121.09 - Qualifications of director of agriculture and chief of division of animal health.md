# Section 121.09 - Qualifications of director of agriculture and chief of division of animal health.

**Effective:** October 17, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 229 - 129th General Assembly

The director of agriculture shall be a person actively identified with agriculture.

The chief of the division of animal health shall be a graduate of a recognized college of veterinary medicine and licensed to practice veterinary medicine and surgery in this state.
