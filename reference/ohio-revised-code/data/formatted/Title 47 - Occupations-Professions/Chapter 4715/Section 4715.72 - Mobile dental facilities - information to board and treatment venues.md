# Section 4715.72 - Mobile dental facilities - information to board and treatment venues.

**Effective:** April 12, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 210 - 133rd General Assembly

Except as provided in section 4715.73 of the Revised Code, a mobile dental facility operator or the operator's representative shall notify the state dental board and all treatment venues not later than fourteen days after either of the following occurs:

(A) There is a change in the address or telephone number of the operator.

(B) The mobile dental facility ceases to operate.

The notice must be provided in writing.
