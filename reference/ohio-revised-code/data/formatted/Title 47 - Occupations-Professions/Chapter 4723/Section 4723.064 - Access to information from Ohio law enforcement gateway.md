# Section 4723.064 - Access to information from Ohio law enforcement gateway.

**Effective:** May 20, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 93 - 129th General Assembly

The board of nursing may access and view, but not alter, information gathered and disseminated through the Ohio law enforcement gateway established under section 109.57 of the Revised Code.
