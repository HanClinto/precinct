# Section 4723.65 - Application for certification as medication aide.

**Effective:** October 24, 2024

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 144 - 135th General Assembly

An individual seeking certification as a medication aide shall apply to the board of nursing on a form prescribed and provided by the board. The application shall be accompanied by a certification fee of fifty dollars.
