# Section 4723.116 - Grants from national council.

**Effective:** January 1, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 3 - 134th General Assembly

The board of nursing may accept grant money from the national council of state boards of nursing to assist in the implementation of sections 4723.11 and 4723.111 of the Revised Code.
