# Section 4723.112 - Multistate license to practice nursing; fee.

**Effective:** January 1, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 3 - 134th General Assembly

A multistate license to practice registered or licensed practical nursing issued pursuant to section 4723.11 of the Revised Code is an option for licensure in addition to a traditional license to practice as a registered nurse or as a licensed practical nurse issued pursuant to section 4723.09 of the Revised Code. The board of nursing may charge a fee in excess of the traditional license fee for issuance of a multistate license. The board shall not pass along to a traditional license applicant or holder any costs associated with entering into or administering the "Nurse Licensure Compact" under section 4723.11 of the Revised Code.
