# Section 4783.20 - Telehealth services.

**Effective:** March 23, 2022

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 122 - 134th General Assembly

A certified Ohio behavior analyst may provide telehealth services in accordance with section 4743.09 of the Revised Code.
