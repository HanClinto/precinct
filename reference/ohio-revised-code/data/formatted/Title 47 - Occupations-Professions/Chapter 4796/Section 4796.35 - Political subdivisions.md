# Section 4796.35 - Political subdivisions.

**Effective:** December 29, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 131 - 134th General Assembly

A political subdivision shall not prohibit an individual who holds a license or government certification issued by a state agency under this chapter from engaging in the respective profession, occupation, or occupational activity in the political subdivision's jurisdiction.
