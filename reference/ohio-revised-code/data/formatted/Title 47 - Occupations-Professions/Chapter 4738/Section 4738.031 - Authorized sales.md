# Section 4738.031 - Authorized sales.

**Effective:** March 23, 2015

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 468 - 130th General Assembly

No person who is not an authorized purchaser shall purchase a salvage motor vehicle from a salvage motor vehicle auction or salvage motor vehicle pool.
