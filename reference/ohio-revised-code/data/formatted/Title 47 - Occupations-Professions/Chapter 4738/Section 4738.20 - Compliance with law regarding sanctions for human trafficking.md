# Section 4738.20 - Compliance with law regarding sanctions for human trafficking.

**Effective:** March 22, 2013

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 247 - 129th General Assembly

The motor vehicle salvage dealer's licensing board shall comply with section 4776.20 of the Revised Code.
