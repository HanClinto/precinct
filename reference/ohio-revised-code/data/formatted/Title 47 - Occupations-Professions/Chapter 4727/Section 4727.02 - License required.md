# Section 4727.02 - License required.

**Effective:** March 28, 2017

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 235 - 131st General Assembly

No person shall act as a pawnbroker, or advertise, transact, or solicit business as a pawnbroker, without first having obtained a license from the superintendent of financial institutions. A person shall obtain a separate license for each place of business at which the person acts or transacts business as a pawnbroker.
