# Section 4727.16 - Advertisements.

**Effective:** April 5, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 467 - 123rd General Assembly

(A) A person licensed as a pawnbroker under this chapter shall state the license number issued by the superintendent of financial institutions in all advertisements, offers, and solicitations.

(B) No person not licensed under this chapter may advertise as a pawnbroker.
