# Section 4727.14 - Exemptions.

**Effective:** April 5, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 467 - 123rd General Assembly

This chapter does not apply to federally insured depository institutions such as a state or federally chartered bank, savings and loan association, or credit union.
