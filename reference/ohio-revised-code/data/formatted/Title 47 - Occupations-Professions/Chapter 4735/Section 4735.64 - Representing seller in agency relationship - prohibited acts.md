# Section 4735.64 - Representing seller in agency relationship - prohibited acts.

**Effective:** December 13, 1996

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 354 - 121st General Assembly

In representing a seller in an agency relationship, no licensee shall do either of the following without the knowledge and consent of the seller:

(A) Extend an offer of subagency to other licensees;

(B) Offer compensation to a broker who represents a purchaser.
