# Section 4741.55 - Operations report.

**Effective:** April 12, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 67 - 133rd General Assembly

The state veterinary medical licensing board shall submit a report to the governor and the general assembly describing the operations of the veterinarian student debt assistance program during the previous biennium. The board shall submit the report by the first day of September of each odd-numbered calendar year and shall include all of the following information in the report:

(A) The total number of veterinarians who applied to participate in the program within the prior twenty-four months;

(B) The total amount of money awarded to program participants within the prior twenty-four months;

(C) The names of the participants who entered into a contract with the board in accordance with section 4741.54 of the Revised Code within the prior twenty-four months;

(D) Any other information the board determines to be pertinent.
