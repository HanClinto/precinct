# Section 4758.43 - Counselor assistant certificate requirements.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

An individual seeking a chemical dependency counselor assistant certificate shall have at least the number of hours specified in rules adopted under section 4758.20 of the Revised Code of training in substance use disorder counseling that meets the requirements specified in those rules.
