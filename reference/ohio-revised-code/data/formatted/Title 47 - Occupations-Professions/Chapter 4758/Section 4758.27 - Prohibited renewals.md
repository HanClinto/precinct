# Section 4758.27 - Prohibited renewals.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

The chemical dependency professionals board shall not renew or restore under section 4758.26 of the Revised Code a registered applicant certificate.
