# Section 4730.101 - Certificate applicant to comply with RC Chapter 4776.

**Effective:** October 9, 2021

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 263 - 133rd General Assembly

In addition to any other eligibility requirement set forth in this chapter, each applicant for a license to practice as a physician assistant shall comply with sections 4776.01 to 4776.04 of the Revised Code.
