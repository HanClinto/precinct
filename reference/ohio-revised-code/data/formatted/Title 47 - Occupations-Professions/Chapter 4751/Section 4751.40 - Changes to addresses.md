# Section 4751.40 - Changes to addresses.

**Effective:** April 6, 2023

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 509 - 134th General Assembly

Each licensed nursing home administrator and licensed health services executive shall report to the board of executives of long-term services and supports any change in any of the following not later than ten days after the change:

(A) The individual's residence mailing address;

(B) The name and address of each place at which the individual practices nursing home administration;

(C) The name and address of each long-term services and supports setting at which the individual serves in a leadership position or directs the practices of others.
