# Section 4713.13 - Injunctions.

**Effective:** January 21, 2018

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 49 - 132nd General Assembly

Whenever in the judgment of the state cosmetology and barber board any individual has engaged in or is about to engage in any acts or practices that constitute a violation of this chapter, or any rule adopted under this chapter, the board may apply to the appropriate court for an order enjoining the acts or practices, and upon a showing by the board that the individual has engaged in the acts or practices, the court shall grant an injunction, restraining order, or other order as may be appropriate.
