# Section 4713.49 - Tanning facility at salon or school.

**Effective:** October 24, 2024

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 158 - 135th General Assembly

The owner or manager of a salon or school that has a permit issued under section 4713.48 of the Revised Code may operate a tanning facility at the salon or school.
