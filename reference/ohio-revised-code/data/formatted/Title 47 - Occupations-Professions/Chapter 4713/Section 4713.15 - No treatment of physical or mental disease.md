# Section 4713.15 - No treatment of physical or mental disease.

**Effective:** April 7, 2003

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 415 - 124th General Assembly

This chapter does not permit any of the services or arts that are part of the practice of a branch of cosmetology to be used for the treatment or cure of a physical or mental disease or ailment.
