# Section 4713.39 - License as independent contractor.

**Effective:** October 24, 2024

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 158 - 135th General Assembly

The state cosmetology and barber board shall issue a license to engage in the practice of a branch of cosmetology as an independent contractor to an applicant who pays the applicable fee; holds a current, valid license for the branch of cosmetology that the applicant practices; and satisfies the conditions for the license established by rules adopted under section 4713.08 of the Revised Code.
