# Section 4713.21 - Reapplying for examination.

**Effective:** September 13, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 213 - 131st General Assembly

Both of the following may apply again under section 4713.20 of the Revised Code for admission to an examination conducted under section 4713.24 of the Revised Code:

(A) An individual who failed to appear for an examination that the individual was previously scheduled to take;

(B) An individual who appeared for a previously scheduled examination but failed to pass it.
