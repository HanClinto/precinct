# Section 2743.08 - Supplies, equipment - compensation of personnel.

**Effective:** January 1, 1975

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 800 - 110th General Assembly

The supreme court shall:

(A) Furnish the clerk of the court of claims with the necessary printing, books, blanks, stationery, and equipment;

(B) Fix the compensation to be paid the clerk and deputy clerks, court reporters, secretaries, and clerical personnel employed by the court of of claims.
