# Section 5921.02 - Divisions of naval militia.

**Effective:** January 11, 1977

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 524 - 111th General Assembly

The Ohio naval militia shall be divided into ship companies and each ship company shall consist of not less than two or more than three divisions.
