# Section 5923.30 - Action against officer unable to account properly for money or property.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

Whenever it is ascertained by the adjutant general that any officer of the organized militia is unable to properly account for the property or moneys in the officer's possession, the adjutant general shall give immediate notice thereof to the attorney general for action against such officer and the officer's bonder, and the attorney general shall bring such action.
