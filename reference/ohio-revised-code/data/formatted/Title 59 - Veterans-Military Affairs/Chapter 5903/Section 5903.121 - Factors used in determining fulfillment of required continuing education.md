# Section 5903.121 - Factors used in determining fulfillment of required continuing education.

**Effective:** September 16, 2014

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 488 - 130th General Assembly

A licensing agency shall consider relevant education, training, or service completed by a licensee as a member of the armed forces in determining whether a licensee has fulfilled required continuing education.
