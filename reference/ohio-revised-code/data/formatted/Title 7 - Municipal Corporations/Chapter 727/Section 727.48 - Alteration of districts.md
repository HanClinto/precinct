# Section 727.48 - Alteration of districts.

**Effective:** January 1, 1962

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 262 - 104th General Assembly

The legislative authority of a municipal corporation may, from time to time rearrange existing districts, establish new districts or sub-districts, or amend the general plan for the district as provided under sections 727.44 to 727.49 , inclusive, of the Revised Code.
