# Section 742.044 - Complaint alleging violation of RC 742.043.

**Effective:** September 30, 2025

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 96 - 136th General Assembly

A complaint alleging a violation of section 742.043 of the Revised Code may be filed in accordance with section 3517.16 of the Revised Code.
