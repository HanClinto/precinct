# Section 742.09 - Attorney general is legal counsel for board.

**Effective:** November 2, 1999

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 222 - 123rd General Assembly

The attorney general shall be the legal counsel of the board of trustees of the Ohio police and fire pension fund.
