# Section 1311.37 - When general employer assumes payment.

**Effective:** October 1, 1953

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 1 - 100th General Assembly

Sections 1311.34 to 1311.36 , inclusive, of the Revised Code apply to and include any laborer who indirectly performs labor for a general employer, or the results of whose labor is immediately enjoyed by a general employer, when such general employer assumes payment of his wages by passing a credit therefor upon his books of account or otherwise. All proceedings under this section shall be the same as provided by such sections.
