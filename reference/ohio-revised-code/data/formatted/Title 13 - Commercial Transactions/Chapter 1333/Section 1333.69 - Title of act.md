# Section 1333.69 - Title of act.

**Effective:** July 20, 1994

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 320 - 120th General Assembly

Sections 1333.61 to 1333.69 of the Revised Code may be cited as the "Uniform Trade Secrets Act."
