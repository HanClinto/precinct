# Section 1307.509 - Adequate compliance with commercial contract - UCC 7-509.

**Effective:** June 29, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 9 - 129th General Assembly

Adequate compliance with commercial contract [UCC 7-509]

Whether a document of title is adequate to fulfill the obligations of a contract for sale, a contract for lease, or the conditions of a letter of credit is determined by sections 1302.01 to 1302.98 , 1305.01 to 1305.16 , and 1310.01 to 1310.78 of the Revised Code.
