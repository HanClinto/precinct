# Section 1309.524 - Delay by filing office - UCC 9-524.

**Effective:** July 1, 2001

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 74 - 124th General Assembly

Delay by the filing office beyond a time limit prescribed by sections 1309.501 to 1309.527 of the Revised Code is excused if:

(A) The delay is caused by interruption of communication or computer facilities, war, emergency conditions, failure of equipment, or other circumstances beyond control of the filing office; and

(B) The filing office exercises reasonable diligence under the circumstances.
