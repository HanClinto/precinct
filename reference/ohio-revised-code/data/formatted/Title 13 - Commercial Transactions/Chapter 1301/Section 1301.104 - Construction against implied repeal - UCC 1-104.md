# Section 1301.104 - Construction against implied repeal - UCC 1-104.

**Effective:** June 29, 2011

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 9 - 129th General Assembly

Construction against implied repeal [UCC 1-104]

Chapters 1301., 1302., 1303., 1304., 1305., 1307., 1308., 1309., and 1310. of the Revised Code being a general act intended as a unified coverage of its subject matter, no part of it shall be deemed to be impliedly repealed by subsequent legislation if such construction can reasonably be avoided.
