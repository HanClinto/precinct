# Section 1304.05 - Payable through or payable at bank - UCC 4-106.

**Effective:** August 19, 1994

**Latest Legislation:** Senate Bill 147 - 120th General Assembly

(A) If an item states that it is "payable through" a bank identified in the item, both of the following apply:

(1) The item designates the bank as a collecting bank and does not by itself authorize the bank to pay the item.

(2) The item may be presented for payment only by or through the bank.

(B) If an item states that it is "payable at" a bank identified in the item, the item is equivalent to a draft drawn on the bank.
