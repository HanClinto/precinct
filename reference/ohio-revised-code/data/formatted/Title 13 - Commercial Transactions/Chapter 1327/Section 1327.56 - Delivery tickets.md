# Section 1327.56 - Delivery tickets.

**Effective:** July 26, 1974

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 203 - 110th General Assembly

Whenever the quantity is determined by the seller, every bulk sale in excess of twenty dollars and every bulk delivery of heating fuel shall be accompanied by a delivery ticket containing the following information:

(A) The name and address of the vendor and purchaser;

(B) The date delivered;

(C) The quantity delivered and the quantity upon which the price is based, if this differs from the delivered quantity;

(D) The identity in the most descriptive terms commercially practicable, including any quality representation made in connection with the sale;

(E) The count of individually wrapped packages, if more than one.
