# Section 1327.291 - Prohibited agreements to gain competitive advantage in sale of milk or milk products.

**Effective:** October 5, 1955

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 547 - 101st General Assembly

No milk dealer shall directly or indirectly through arrangements with other persons provide refrigerated bulk milk dispensers or parts or accessories thereof to any other person as a means of awarding a premium, rebate, or discount in order to gain competitive advantage in the sale of milk or milk products. Whenever any such item is provided or sold at less than its prevailing wholesale price, it shall be presumed it was provided as a premium, rebate, or discount.
