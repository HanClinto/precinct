# Section 1327.502 - Registration required.

**Effective:** March 23, 2016

**Latest Legislation:** House Bill 131 - 131st General Assembly

A service person who is employed by a commercially used weighing and measuring device servicing agency shall register with the director of agriculture in accordance with rules adopted under section 1327.50 of the Revised Code.
